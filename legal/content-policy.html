Content and Malware Policy

Software listed on Noodle must be what it says it is, must not harm the computer or the person using it, and must be lawful to distribute. Every release is scanned before listing, but a scan reduces risk and does not remove it, so the site never promises that an app is "malware-free."

Not allowed

  • Malware of any kind: viruses, ransomware, trojans, spyware, keyloggers, cryptominers or botnet clients, credential stealers, and anything that hides its behavior.
  • Software that collects personal data without clear disclosure and consent, or sends it somewhere not described in the listing.
  • Bundled adware, toolbars, or installers that add software the user did not choose.
  • Cheats, key generators, license crackers, or pirated software.
  • Software built for fraud, harassment, stalking, or unauthorized access to other people's systems.
  • Anything unlawful to distribute, including sexual content involving minors, which is reported to the authorities as the law requires.
  • Impersonation of another company, app or developer, or misleading names and screenshots.
  • Misleading claims about what the software does.

How scanning works

  1. Each upload is scanned with [VirusTotal and/or ClamAV] before it can be listed.
  2. Automated analysis inspects the file and its contents (including hard-coded keys and passwords), runs it in an isolated test environment, and compares what it does with its listing.
  3. A release that fails is rejected with a reason, and the developer may fix and resubmit.
  4. Approved releases are published with their SHA-256 hash.
  5. Published releases may be re-scanned later as detection databases update. A release that later fails is delisted.

Who looks at it. Most releases are reviewed by automated tools alone, and a person looks at the rest. Which route a release takes depends on the scan result and the developer's history:

Scan result What happens
Clear, from a developer with a clean track record Approved and published automatically. A small random share is checked by a person anyway
Clear, from a new developer Held for at least 24 hours and checked by a person before publishing
Anything unusual (packed code, odd network activity, one engine flags it) Sent to a person
Malware or other serious problem Rejected automatically and a strike is recorded. A person decides whether to ban

A developer earns the clean track record after two approved releases and no strikes in 12 months. Every decision, automatic or human, is recorded with its reason. Automated approval is not a guarantee of safety, and Noodle may remove a release at any time.

Enforcement

Violation Result
Minor (mislabeled listing, unclear data disclosure) Warning and a time limit to fix
Repeat or serious Delisting, earnings withheld during review
Malware, fraud, illegal content Immediate delisting, account ban, users notified, reported where required

Two warnings in 12 months lead to suspension. Developers may appeal once by email within [14] days.

Reporting

Anyone can report a listing through the Report button on every app page or at [abuse@noodletechnology.com]. Reports about malware or illegal content are reviewed first, with a target response of [24] hours.
